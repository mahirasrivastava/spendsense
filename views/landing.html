Features

Everything a student budget actually needs

Not a spreadsheet with extra steps. SpendSense does the arithmetic, the forecasting and the awkward roommate maths for you.

Month-end forecast

Your daily pace is projected across the full month, so you find out you are heading over budget with time left to do something about it.

Unusual spend detection

Every charge is compared against your own history for that category. Anything well above your normal gets flagged automatically.

Category limits

Set a cap per category and watch the progress bars. You get a warning at 80% and a clear alert once you cross the line.

Roommate splitting

Log shared costs, split them evenly, and get the shortest list of transfers that clears every balance between everyone.

Recurring expenses

Rent, mess fees and subscriptions post themselves when they come due, so your forecast is never missing the predictable stuff.

Charts and CSV export

Category breakdown, a seven-day bar chart and a six-month trend line. Export any month to CSV when you need the raw numbers.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my financial data private?

Yes. SpendSense runs on your own machine and stores everything in a local SQLite database file. Nothing is sent to a third-party service, and each account only ever sees its own data.

How does the forecast work?

It divides what you have spent so far by the number of days elapsed, then projects that daily pace across the whole month. If the projection lands over your budget you are warned early, while you can still change course.

What counts as unusual spending?

Each expense is compared with the mean and standard deviation of your own history in that category. Anything above ₹300 that sits more than two standard deviations out, or more than double your average, gets flagged.

How is the roommate settle-up calculated?

Every shared expense adds to what the payer is owed and subtracts each person's share. The balances are then matched largest creditor against largest debtor, which produces the fewest transfers needed to clear everyone.

Can I get my data out?

Any month exports to CSV from the Expenses page, with dates, descriptions, categories, amounts and notes.
